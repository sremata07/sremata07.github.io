Coursera is an online educational platform. The course I was taking on Coursera was created by Google for a Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate. 

This online course was shown to me by my academic advisor so I started it during the Summer of 2026. While I have not finished the full course, I am still working on it as my Fall 2026 semester starts and I will most likely finish it by October. As this is a class I am unsure if I am allowed to show pictures showing the course materials, however if you are currently a student at the University of Hawaii at Mānoa you should be able to sign up for some Coursera courses for free using your @hawaii.edu email. 

I find Coursera to be a generally helpful website with lots of hands-on work, quizzes and educational videos. I learned a lot about Cybersecurity from its basics, how companies like Google handle data breaches, what specialists do during work, etc.

If you are interested, UH Mānoa student or not, you can find the Google Coursera programs here:
https://www.coursera.org/google-career-certificates
