## What is software engineering?

Software engineering is a broad concept that requires people to, well, engineer software. Specifically, one is expected to develop, test, and maintain software. First, you need to develop the idea for the software, develop the code, develop the visuals, etc. Next, you need to test the software. Testing is important because you can not release an unfinished, buggy project. Finally, you need to maintain the software. Time will always march on, and software needs to be maintained so that it can continue to work into the unforseeable future. 

## What do I hope to gain?

Judging by these requirements, software engineering is a lifelong learning experience. Software engineering would require constant developing, testing, and maintaining, else your software will eventually become decrepit. So, what I hope to gain is a lifelong enjoyment from developing. I plan to go into cybersecurity, and generally I plan to work in the computer science field. Game development, software engineering, cybersecurity, information and teechnology, all of it intrigues me and I will not be able to survive if I do not enjoy what I do. 
What else I hope to gain from this class is being able to think on the spot. With tight deadlines and WODs that provide time-limited challenges, I will be forced to code using what I can think up of on the spot. This has always been a sore spot for me as I despise to do things without insructions, it's part of the reason I even got into computer science in the first place. To me, coding, and cybersecurity especially, always felt "solved" and that I would not have to flounder around on my own.
